Drop year mindset - the real game

  • You have 10-12 hrs per day - use this to go deeper, not just faster. A student who masters integration beats one who rushed through 3 extra chapters.
  • Pre-read every coaching topic the night before class. This turns coaching into a powerful Q&A session instead of a first encounter.
  • Write a post-coaching recap within 1 hour of getting home - do not skip this. Retention drops by 70% if you wait until evening.
  • The biggest drop year trap is an inconsistent July-August. One lazy month undoes weeks of good work. Protect those months fiercely.
  • Take Sunday mornings off completely - rest is not wasted time, it is what makes 10-hr days sustainable for 9 months.

Physics - board exam strategy

  • Every NCERT derivation must be written from memory with all steps - boards give zero partial credit if steps are skipped.
  • Ray optics and AC circuits carry the highest marks in Section C - master these before everything else.
  • Numericals: always write the formula, substitution, and unit in separate lines. A unit error costs full marks on a 3-mark question.
  • Semiconductor logic gates appear every single year. Do not skip Ch 14 even if your coaching does not cover it in depth.
  • With drop year time, solve ALL NCERT Exemplar problems - boards increasingly pull from Exemplar.

Chemistry - how to lock in full marks

  • Name reactions are non-negotiable: Aldol, Cannizzaro, Reimer-Tiemann, Wolff-Kishner, Sandmeyer, Gabriel synthesis, Kolbe. Write all 7 from memory daily in October.
  • Electrochemistry numericals (Nernst equation, Kohlrausch) and kinetics (first-order rate law) appear every year - drill these weekly.
  • p-block: the anomalous properties of the first element of each group is a guaranteed 1-mark question every year.
  • With your extra time, practise 10 organic conversions every day from October. You can finish every standard conversion twice over before exams.
  • Coordination compounds IUPAC naming: 3 guaranteed marks - practise 5 compounds per day until it is automatic.

Mathematics - path to 100/100

  • Integration alone carries 20+ marks. Master every standard form, substitution method, by-parts (ILATE), and properties of definite integrals before October.
  • Differential equations - variable separable and linear first-order appear every single year without exception.
  • In the exam, attempt Case Study and Assertion-Reason questions first - they are the easiest marks and require no heavy calculation.
  • Probability: Bayes theorem appears in some form in almost every paper. Solve 20+ variety problems in November.
  • Write every step even when you are unsure - boards give step marking. A wrong final answer with correct method still earns 3 out of 5 marks.
General exam strategy
Start CBSE Previous Year Papers from September 2026 (one month earlier than school students). Chapter-wise first, full papers from November onwards.
Maintain an Error Log. Every mistake in practice is written down. Review it every Sunday morning before your rest time.
Make one-page handwritten formula sheets per chapter as you finish them. From January, read them every morning before anything else.
In the exam hall: spend the first 15 minutes reading the full paper, planning the order, and marking easy questions. Never start writing immediately.
The week before each board paper: light revision only, no new topics, 8 hrs sleep. Over-studying the final week is the single most common drop year mistake.
